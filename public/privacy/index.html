Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

This policy explains how [Company legal name] ("Posteeze", "we", "us"), [Registered address], Dubai, United Arab Emirates, handles personal data when you use the Posteeze website and app (the "Service"). Contact us at support@posteeze.com.

What we collect

  • Account data: your name, email address and password (stored hashed), and the workspace you belong to.
  • Connected social accounts: when you connect a channel, the platform gives us an access token and basic profile details (account name, ID, profile picture). We never receive your social media password.
  • Content you create: posts, captions, images, videos, comments, schedules and settings.
  • Analytics from platforms: metrics the platforms return for your connected accounts and posts, such as followers, reach and engagement.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type and logs needed to run and secure the Service.

How we use it

  • To publish and schedule the content you ask us to, on the accounts you connect.
  • To show you analytics for your own accounts and posts.
  • To provide AI writing and image features when you use them.
  • To run, secure, support and improve the Service, and to contact you about your account.

We use data from connected platforms only to provide these features to you. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use platform data for advertising or to build profiles of you.

Platform-specific terms

  • YouTube: Posteeze uses YouTube API Services. By connecting YouTube you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke our access at any time from your Google security settings.
  • Meta (Facebook, Instagram, Threads), LinkedIn, X, TikTok, Pinterest and others: access is granted through each platform's official login and can be revoked in that platform's settings or by disconnecting the channel in Posteeze.

Who we share it with

  • Social platforms you connect, to publish your content and fetch your analytics.
  • Service providers that host and run the Service on our behalf, including cloud hosting and, when you use AI features, an AI model provider that processes the prompt you submit.
  • Authorities where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. When you disconnect a channel we delete its access token. When you delete your account, we delete your account data, connected-account tokens and content within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it longer.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, and object to or restrict its processing. Email support@posteeze.com or follow the steps on our data deletion page.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access tokens are stored securely, and access to production systems is limited to staff who need it.

Children

The Service is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

We will post changes to this policy here and update the date above. For significant changes we will notify you by email or in the app.
