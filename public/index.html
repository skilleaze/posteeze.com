FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does it really post by itself?

Yes. Once your plan is set, Posteeze creates and schedules your posts and publishes them on time. You can review each post first, or turn on full autopilot.

How do I approve posts?

Choose during setup: in the Posteeze app, by email, on WhatsApp, or all three. Each preview comes with Approve and Request changes buttons, so you can sign off from your phone in seconds.

How does it know my business?

During setup you describe your business, audience, offers and tone. Posteeze turns that into a brand brief and uses it for every idea, design and caption.

What kind of videos can it make?

Short AI videos, cinematic clips and motion graphics sized for Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts. The number per month depends on your plan.

Do I need to share my social media passwords?

No. You connect each account through the network's own official login, and you can disconnect it any time.

Can I use my own Higgsfield, Magnific or OpenAI account?

Yes. Your plan includes AI credits, and you can connect your own accounts to create more.

What currency do you charge in?

All plans are priced and billed in US dollars.
