FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need to share my social media passwords?

No. You connect each account through the network's own official login, and you can disconnect it any time.

Which networks can I post to?

Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, TikTok, YouTube, Threads, Pinterest, Google Business Profile, Reddit, Bluesky, Mastodon, Telegram, Discord and more.

Can the AI write in my brand's voice?

Yes. Give it an idea and it drafts captions for each platform. You edit or approve before anything is scheduled.

Can my team or clients approve posts first?

Yes. Invite them to a workspace and drafts wait for review before they are published.

Can I manage more than one brand?

Yes. Each brand or client gets its own workspace, channels and members.
