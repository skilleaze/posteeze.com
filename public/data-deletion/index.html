Data Deletion

Last updated 26 November 2026

You can remove a single connected account, or delete everything Posteeze holds about you.

Disconnect one social account

  1. Sign in to Posteeze.
  2. Open the channel's menu (the three dots next to it) and choose Delete Channel. If the channel still has scheduled posts, delete those first.
  3. We delete that channel's access token straight away. You can also revoke access from the platform's own settings, for example Facebook: Settings > Apps and Websites.

Delete your account and all data

Email support@posteeze.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my Posteeze data". We confirm by email and delete your account, connected-account tokens, posts and media within 30 days.

Requests from Facebook or Instagram

If you remove Posteeze from your Facebook or Instagram settings, we delete the data we received through that connection. To check the status of a request, email support@posteeze.com.
